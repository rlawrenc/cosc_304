You must use **AI tools** (e.g., ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) to research or assist in implementing the feature, while ensuring you understand all submitted code and results.

## Deliverables

Submit your updated code (development stream) or Excel file (analysis stream) and a **short document containing the following three paragraphs, a screenshot demonstrating the feature, and a brief explanation of how it works**.

### **1. Feature Plan (3 marks)**
Describe the idea or feature you selected, why it fits your project, and the steps you planned to take to add it.

### **2. How AI Was Used (3 marks)**
Explain how you used AI and what AI systems were used including the questions you asked. Describe at least one way you tested or verified the AI-generated suggestions, code, or results.

### **3. Reflection (3 marks)**
Reflect on your use of AI: what worked well, what didn’t, what you learned, and how you might use AI differently in the future. Did the AI save you time?
